Explain what is directly observed, what may reasonably be researched next, and what remains unknown.\n\n## Evidence policy\n- The evidence ledger is the complete factual source of truth for this response.\n- Never add facts from general knowledge or assumptions.\n- Never treat the deterministic score as proof of security risk, buying intent, urgency, vulnerability, compromise, or a confirmed security need.\n- Distinguish **Observed** facts from **Possible interpretation**.\n- If a requested fact is absent, put it under **Missing information**.\n\n## Required output\n1. **Observed signals** — only fields and deterministic signals in the ledger.\n2. **Possible interpretation** — cautious research hypotheses, explicitly labelled as such.\n3. **Missing information** — important unknowns such as industry, company size, business priorities, security stack, ownership, incidents, or buying intent when not present.\n4. **Suggested research question** — one concrete next research step that could be answered with additional evidence.\n\n## Input evidence ledger\n{{evidence_ledger}}\n
